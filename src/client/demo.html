What to try

  • Set 8 ticks per second and click around. Age of Empires IV is reported to run at this rate. Then set 30, our target.
  • Turn smoothing off to see the simulation as it really moves, one tick at a time. With smoothing on, the display blends between ticks at your monitor's full rate.
  • Add input delay to feel online play. Local play uses 0; a good connection needs about 2 ticks, a poor one 4 or more.
  • appears the instant you click, whatever the settings: feedback never waits for the simulation. move only when the order's tick arrives. "Last order" measures that gap.
  • "Verify by replay" re-runs the whole session from its recorded orders and compares checksums. A match is what makes replays, saved games and lockstep multiplayer possible.
