Spearmen stop horsemen, horsemen ride down archers, archers shoot spearmen before they close. Soldiers left alone guard where they stand; a plain move ignores the enemy.

You see only what your people see. Ground you have left stays on the map as you last saw it; ground you have never seen is dark, and nothing can be built there. Lift fog shows everything, for testing.

You lead the blue side, in the north-west. Red has no general yet: its villagers work, its guards and town centre defend, and a raiding party sets out for your town every minute and a half from the third minute, a little larger each time. Pull down its town centre to win.
