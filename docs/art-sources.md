# Openly licensed art sources: survey of 11 November 2026

A survey of art we could legally ship in the game, including a commercial
release. Licences change; re-open the linked page before relying on any row.
Nothing here is legal advice.

## How to read the verification marks

- **[D]** The source's own licence page or file was fetched directly.
- **[S]** A direct fetch was blocked, so the source's page was read through a
  search-engine extract. Provisional: re-open the URL before relying on it.
- **[M]** Memory or inference, not verified.

Blocked sources, so [S] only: Mixamo FAQ, Poly Pizza, Freesound, Sonniss, The
Met, Google Fonts FAQ, Rijksmuseum policy.

Independently re-checked after the survey: Kenney's support page, the
Quaternius FAQ and the Quaternius Ultimate Fantasy RTS pack page. All three
state CC0 as recorded below.

## 9. Licence traps

### What CC BY-SA 3.0 obliges if 0 A.D. assets ship in a closed commercial game

Commercial use itself is allowed [D]. The conditions:

1. **Attribution.** Credit "Wildfire Games", link the licence and
   wildfiregames.com, and identify adapted works as adaptations [D].
2. **Share-alike on adaptations.** Anything "based upon" the asset may be
   distributed only under CC BY-SA. A reasonable reading [M] is that this
   covers retextures, re-rigs, decimated or kit-bashed meshes, new animations
   built on their skeletons, and icons rendered from the models.
3. **Format conversion.** Converting .dae to .glb is not itself an adaptation
   per the CC FAQ [D], but the file stays CC BY-SA.
4. **No extra terms.** A licence agreement cannot forbid players extracting,
   modifying or reselling those assets [D].
5. **No DRM on them.** "Effective technological measures" that restrict those
   rights are not allowed [D]. Encrypted asset bundles, a DRM wrapper or
   console packaging are a real conflict.
6. **Breach.** The licence terminates automatically under 3.0 [D]; 4.0 adds a
   30-day cure [D].
7. **Code.** On the licence text, share-alike does not reach the game's code:
   it binds adaptations, and a "Collection" of separate works is expressly not
   one [D]. Whether a whole game counts as an adaptation of art it displays is
   an unsettled grey zone [M].
8. **No source-file duty.** CC BY-SA does not require releasing .blend files.

Separately, do not copy 0 A.D. templates, scripts, maps or shaders: those are
GPL v2+ [D].

### Other traps

- **NC and ND terms.** Freesound CC BY-NC, Tabletop Audio, Bandai Namco mocap,
  Bodleian, British Museum, and many Sketchfab models.
- **GPL-licensed art** (Wesnoth, many OpenGameArt items). How the GPL applies
  to game art is unclear; the OpenGameArt FAQ tells closed-source developers to
  be cautious [D]. Avoid.
- **CC BY 3.0 DRM clause.** Applies to game-icons.net and many OpenGameArt
  items. Prefer CC0 or OGA-BY, or keep those files unencrypted [M].
- **"Free but not open".** Mixamo, Sonniss, BlenderKit Royalty Free [S] and the
  Unity Asset Store standard licence [S] allow shipping in a game but not
  publishing raw files. That rules out a public repository, and individually
  fetchable files in a web build are a risk [M].
- **Engine-locked content.** Epic's "UE-Only Content" may be used only with
  Unreal [S]. That matters while the engine is undecided.
- **Fonts.** "Free for personal use" fonts on aggregator sites are not usable.
- **AI-generated and unclear-provenance packs.** itch.io, Sketchfab and Steam
  all require AI disclosure [S]. A CC0 label on such a pack waives only what
  the uploader owns.
- **Ripped assets.** Sketchfab licences are uploader-declared with no warranty
  [D]. Reject models resembling commercial games whatever licence is stated.
- **Museum sites.** Only images carrying the CC0 or open-access mark are
  covered; the same site's other images are often barred from commercial use.

## 10. Gaps

- **Animated cavalry.** Nothing under CC0 or CC BY combines a rider, tack and
  mounted-combat animation. The pieces exist (a CC0 animated horse, CC0
  humanoid rigs); the saddle and the riding and mounted-attack clips must be
  authored. The only exception is 0 A.D. and its mods, under share-alike.
- **Non-European architecture.** Nothing permissive. Under share-alike there
  are Han Chinese, Mauryan, Persian, Kushite, Umayyad, Byzantine and
  steppe-yurt sets, all ancient or pre-1000 and in a realistic style. Nothing
  usable was found for Japan, West Africa, the high-medieval Islamic world,
  Tang-to-Ming China, medieval India or the Mongols.
- **Historically accurate late-medieval armour.** Open character packs are
  generic fantasy. Good CC0 reference exists (Met, Cleveland including 3D
  scans, Art Institute of Chicago, ColBase for Japan) for modelling our own.
- **Also missing:** gunpowder units and artillery; period ships beyond dromon,
  knarr and pirate-era hulls; animated siege engines outside share-alike; CC0
  camels and elephants; unit voice lines in period languages; region-specific
  music.
- **Not a gap:** villager work animations (farming, fishing, tool use).

## 11. Recommended starting kit (one European civilization, no share-alike)

1. **Quaternius (CC0, glTF):** Ultimate Fantasy RTS for tiered buildings,
   walls, towers and docks; Universal Base Characters plus Modular Outfits for
   units; Universal Animation Library 1 and 2; Ultimate Animated Animal Pack
   for the horse and huntable or herdable animals; Stylized Nature MegaKit.
2. **Kenney (CC0):** Castle Kit for siege engines and gates; UI Pack –
   Adventure and Fantasy UI Borders; RPG Audio, Impact Sounds, Interface
   Sounds.
3. **Icons:** game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0, keep an attribution ledger), or stay
   fully CC0 with Kenney Board Game Icons or our own SVG set.
4. **Fonts (OFL):** Cinzel for titles, IM Fell English or EB Garamond for body,
   Texturina or UnifrakturMaguntia for display.
5. **Terrain materials:** ambientCG or Poly Haven (CC0).
6. **Audio:** CC0 items from OpenGameArt; optionally Incompetech or Nakarada
   (CC BY 4.0) and Sonniss for depth.
7. **Encyclopedia and loading art:** Met Open Access and Getty Open Content.

This kit is CC0 plus OFL, with optional CC BY, so the only obligations are a
credits screen and bundled font licences. Its one hole is cavalry.

Use one character family only; KayKit's chibi proportions do not mix with
Quaternius's [M]. Treat 0 A.D. and Millennium A.D. as reference unless we
deliberately accept share-alike for an unencrypted, separately licensed asset
set.
